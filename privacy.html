Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data we process when you visit triageagent.dev, join a waitlist or write to us, why we process it, and what rights you have. We keep it short because we collect little.

1. Who is responsible

The controller is [Legal entity name], [registered address], [country] ("we", "us"). Write to us at privacy@triageagent.dev for anything in this policy.

2. What we collect

  • Waitlist. Your email address, the plan you chose (individual or team), your company name if you give one on the team form, the time you consented and the version of the consent wording you saw. Joining is a two-step process: we email you a confirmation link and add you to the list only when you open it. For this we store a random confirmation code, kept only as a hash, and the time you confirmed.
  • Email to us. If you write to sales or privacy, we keep the message and your address to answer it.
  • Technical data. When the form is submitted, your IP address is used in memory to limit abuse and is not written to our logs or database. Our website host keeps ordinary server logs (see section 6).

We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking on triageagent.dev. The agent specification page (/agent.html) loads fonts from Google Fonts and code highlighting from the Cloudflare cdnjs network, so those providers receive your IP address when you open that page.

3. Why we use it, and on what basis

PurposeLegal basis
Tell you when your plan opens, and offer the individual free trialYour consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time.
Answer messages and sales enquiriesSteps at your request before a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).
Keep the site and form secure and free of abuseOur legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising or take decisions about you by automated means.

4. Who receives it

Only the providers that host our website, run the waitlist service and send our emails on our behalf. They are listed on the subprocessors page and are bound by data processing terms.

5. Transfers outside the EEA and UK

Our website is hosted by a United States company. Where personal data goes to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • Signups you never confirm: deleted automatically 48 hours after you submit the form.
  • Confirmed waitlist entries: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, and in any case no longer than 24 months after you joined, unless you have become a customer.
  • Emails to us: as long as needed to deal with your request, then up to 12 months.
  • Website server logs: as long as our host retains them.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to give you access to your data, correct it, erase it, restrict or stop its use, or give you a copy in a portable format. You may withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect what we did before. Write to privacy@triageagent.dev and we will reply within one month. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

8. Security

Traffic to the site and the waitlist service is encrypted. Access to the waitlist is limited to the people who run it, and we keep only what this policy lists.

9. Children

Triage Agent is a business tool and is not aimed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If we change what we collect or why, we will update this page and its date, and ask again for consent where the law requires it. When customer accounts open, this policy will be extended to cover them.
